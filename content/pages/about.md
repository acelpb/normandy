Hi, I'm Augustin Borsu. I live in Belgium with my partner and our two kids, and I like taking things apart to see how they work, then putting them back together slightly differently.

## By day

I'm an IT tribe lead for artificial intelligence. In a field with a lot of hype, my job is mostly to keep people from jumping from one tool to another, and make sure that we ansewr real business needs with as little complexity as needed. I care about practical results and automating the boring parts, and I believe the simplest solution that works is usually the right one. Two quotes keep me honest:

> Simple is better than complex. Complex is better than complicated.
> <br>*The Zen of Python*

> Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.
> <br>*Antoine de Saint-Exupéry*

## By night (and weekends)

- **Renovating our house.** Four years in, the novelty has worn off, yet there is always something left to do.
- **Cycling.** Nothing like feeling the open road and the wind in my hair.
- **Playing Go.** A game where the rules fit on a napkin and the strategy doesn't fit in a lifetime.
- **Railroad Tycoon III,** when I need a break from the vicissitudes of life. Some people meditate; I build railways to nowhere and watch the little trains go.

I speak French, English and I dabble in Dutch, German, Polish, and Russian so if you write to me in any of those, I'll answer. Probably even in the right one.

## About this site

This is my sandbox: the place where I try new technologies and write down what I learned, mostly so future me doesn't have to work it out again. You'll find notes on self-hosting (the site runs on a [NixOS](https://nixos.org) server I manage myself, alongside our family Nextcloud), data infrastructure, and hardware tinkering.

It's a static site generated with [Pelican](https://getpelican.com/) from Markdown files. The pages don't use trackers or cookies. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/acelpb/normandy), and so is the [server configuration](https://github.com/acelpb/acelpb).
